HOW TO PLAY

DEFEND THE KEEP. Deploy troops with the unit buttons, then click the battlefield. Kills earn Royal Purse; a small amount also arrives organically.

HEROES. A soldier with 4 kills becomes a named Hero with an aura. Only living Heroes return for the next level. Veterans continue earning upgrades.

STRATEGY. Formation organizes nearby troops. Fall Back recalls them. Help sends nearby troops toward a threatened point. Injured Troop Retreat sends wounded defenders to heal. Toggle Aggressive or Defensive stance to control how far troops advance.

TOWERS & WALLS. Towers cost 20. Walls cost 10, snap together, and can attach to towers. Enemies walk around openings or batter through blocked defenses. Friendly gates open automatically.

CAVALRY. Knights deal 50% extra damage to archers and fire archers.

DRAGON. Costs 100. For about 9 seconds use the ARROW KEYS to steer it. Fire blasts enemies within about 80 pixels; then the Dragon flies away.

LEVELS. Survive the full enemy army, choose a Royal Workshop improvement, then face a stronger assault. Enemy HP, damage and attack rate rise each level, but marching speed does not.

NATIONS. Your chosen realm grants a special bonus. The invading nation is randomly selected and receives its corresponding advantage.

OTHER. Music can be toggled at upper left. Press Q for Developer Mode.
